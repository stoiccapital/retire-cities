# Retire Cities

Where should you retire? 45 cities ranked for comfortable later life.

Opinionated. No ads. No tracking. One HTML file, no backend.

## What it ranks

- **Healthcare** — quality + accessibility to foreigners
- **Visa path** — real retirement visas (D7, Pensionado, NHR, MM2H, SRRV…)
- **Cost of living** — retired-couple monthly budget (USD)
- **Pension tax** — effective rate on foreign pension income
- **Safety** — day-to-day + night-time
- **Climate** — year-round comfort
- **English** — how far you get without the local language
- **Expat community** — social connection without reinventing yourself at 65
- **Air quality** — health matters more at this stage
- **Walkability** — car-free life is easier on everyone
- **Flight hub** — visits home + grandkids

## Opinions baked in

- Portugal's D7 + NHR + Mediterranean climate puts Lisbon and Algarve near the top
- Italy's 7 % flat retiree tax (southern towns) makes Lecce punch above its weight
- Greece's 7 % flat for 15 years is one of the best tax regimes in the world
- Panama Pensionado is still a benchmark — $1 000/mo pension gets you in
- Thailand's O-A visa + great hospitals keeps Chiang Mai competitive, air quality drags it
- US cities are deliberately absent — Medicare locks you in domestically
- Dubai scores high on tax + safety, climate crushes it May–Oct
